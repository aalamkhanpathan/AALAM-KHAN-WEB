IRON MAN


     



  Iron Man is a superhero from American comics, 
appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, 
created by Stan Lee, Jack Kirby, Larry Lieber, and Don Heck.
The character is most famously known for his role as Tony Stark,
a billionaire industrialist and inventor who uses a high-tech suit of 
armor to fight evil.
The first Iron Man movie, starring Robert Downey Jr. and directed by Jon Favreau,
was released in 2008 and became a major cinematic success..

    
         

     
IRON MAN DETAILS
                                                                                 
     
     
    
     
  •  ORIGIN: 
       Tony Stark, a wealthy and brilliant inventor, 
         is captured and forced to build a weapon. Instead, 
         he uses his intelligence to design and construct a powerful,
         high-tech suit of armor to escape and fight against evil. 
        
    • 
          
  •  Creators: 
          The character was co-created by writer Stan Lee, 
            scripter Larry Lieber, and artists Don Heck and Jack Kirby. 
    • 
          
  • Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU): 
                           The 2008 film Iron Man, directed by Jon Favreau,
                                 introduced the character to a wide audience and launched the interconnected MCU. Robert Downey Jr.
                               's portrayal of Tony Stark is highly acclaimed.  
    • 
          
  •  Powers: 
        Iron Man's suit provides him with superhuman strength, flight, and a range of powerful weapons, 
          making him a formidable force.
